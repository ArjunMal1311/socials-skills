# Content calendar

A plan the creator can keep. Ask first, plan second.

## Ask
- Posts per week they can really make, and how many minutes they have to film and edit each.
- Which formats they can make (talking head, screen recording, carousel, voiceover).
- The ideas they have approved, with share blocks that pass (see share-check).
- Any days they will not post.

## Plan
- Only schedule ideas that pass the share check and have findings.
- **Rotate the angle**: do not run the same topic or hook type twice in a row. Cap a topic at three posts, then
  change the promise, not just the look. Repeats of one message tend to fade; it is a pattern we saw on one
  account, not a law.
- **Change one thing at a time** against the creator's recent posts (format, hook type, length, topic), so a
  result can be read.
- Mark which posts need the creator's face and which can be made without filming.
- Mix reels and carousels if both are available.

## Output
A table: date, format, idea, hook, share driver, what to film or make, keyword (or none), the one thing being
tested. Then a line on total filming time for the week.

## Rules
- Never plan more than the creator said they can make.
- Nothing is posted by this skill. The creator approves and posts every item.
