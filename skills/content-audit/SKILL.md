# Content audit

Compare each post to the account's **own** usual views, not to other accounts.

## Steps
1. Load the posts: url, date, format (reel, carousel), topic, hook type, views, and shares, saves and follows if
   the user has them from Insights. Ask for Insights numbers; a scrape does not have them.
2. Set aside posts under 4 days old. They are still gaining views.
3. Work out the account's median views from the settled posts (outlier-finder has the maths).
4. Tag each post with format, topic and hook type. Ask the user when unsure instead of guessing.
5. Group by each tag. For every group with 3 or more posts, report the median and how it compares to the
   account median.

## Output
- **Do more of:** groups clearly above the account median, with the posts as proof.
- **Stop:** groups clearly below it after 3 or more tries.
- **Test next:** one change at a time, drawn from the best group, with how you will judge it.
- A line saying how many posts were used and how many were left out, and why.

## Rules
- Fewer than 3 posts in a group is an anecdote, not a pattern. Say so.
- Correlation only. Never write "X caused Y".
- Do not mix reels and carousels in one median.
