# On-screen text

Many viewers watch on mute. The text must carry the video on its own.

## Three layers
1. **Frame-0 headline**: the claim, 6 words or fewer, on screen from the first frame.
2. **Captions**: the spoken words in chunks of one to three words, timed to the voice. Highlight the word being
   spoken.
3. **Callouts**: a short label on the proof ("no code", "2 minutes", the tool name) that appears when the line is
   said, not before.

## Rules
- Text is large and sits in the middle of the frame. The top ~250 px and bottom ~420 px of a 1080x1920 frame are
  covered by the app's interface.
- Never put a different claim in the text than the voice says.
- Every number in text is also in the findings (see research-first-script).
- High contrast. Check it on a phone-sized preview, not only on a monitor.
- No more than one callout on screen at a time.

## Output
A table: time, spoken words, caption chunks, callout (or none). Flag any beat with no text and no change on
screen for more than 3 seconds.
