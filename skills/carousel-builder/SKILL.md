# Carousel builder

One promise, a few proofs, one ask. Each slide earns the swipe to the next.

## Structure (8 slides is a good default, 6 to 10 works)
1. **Promise**: the outcome in 8 words or fewer, readable as a thumbnail on the grid.
2. to 6. **Proof**: one idea per slide, a real screenshot or real output on each, one line of text.
7. **Objection**: the doubt a skeptical viewer has ("what does it cost?", "does it need code?"), answered with a fact.
8. **Ask**: one action, and what the viewer gets for it.

## Rules
- Max 25 words a slide. If a slide needs more, it is two slides.
- Real screenshots only. No mock-ups of results you did not get.
- Every number needs a row in the findings (see research-first-script) and the share block must pass
  (see share-check).
- Slide 1 and slide 8 matter most: slide 1 earns the stop, slide 8 earns the action.
- Write alt text for each slide.

## Output
A table: slide number, text, what is on the slide, source of any fact. Then the caption (see
caption-and-keyword).

## Note
On one growth account we studied (28 carousels vs 121 reels), carousels drew about twice the likes and comments
of reels. That is one account; test it on yours before you trust it.
