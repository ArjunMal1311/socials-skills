# Reel audit

Check the actual file, not the script. For every point write PASS, FIX or WAIVE, with the timestamp or frame
that proves it. A WAIVE needs a reason in the creator's own words.

## The ten points
1. Frame 0 is already moving, not black and not a fade-in.
2. Frame 0 carries the claim in the picture or in big text (works on mute).
3. The first spoken word lands within about 0.5 s.
4. The claim is clear by about 1.5 s.
5. One idea only. If you can name two topics, it is two reels.
6. The picture changes about every 3 s, and each change matches the line being said.
7. Text is large, inside the middle of the frame (the top ~250 px and bottom ~420 px of a 1080x1920 frame are
   covered by the app's interface), and on screen long enough to read.
8. Every number said or shown is true and has a source (see research-first-script).
9. One ask at the end, and what it promises really exists.
10. Audio is clear and loudness is about -14 LUFS, with no clipping.

## Output
A table of ten rows, then the three fixes that matter most, in order, each with the exact timestamp.

## Rules
- These are rules of thumb. When the creator's own results disagree with one (see reel-tracking), say so.
- Do not rewrite the reel. Point at what to change.
- If you cannot open the file, say so. Do not audit from the description.
