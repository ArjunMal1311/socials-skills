# Reel analyzer

Study one reel so the shape can be reused. Never the lines, never the footage.

## Get the material
1. Download the video (yt-dlp with the user's own browser cookies if needed) and get a transcript with word
   timestamps (Whisper).
2. Pull frames at 0 s, 1 s, 2.5 s, 25 %, 50 %, 75 % and the last second, and look at them.

If you cannot get the video, say so and work from the caption only. Do not describe frames you did not see.

## Write the breakdown
| Part | What to record |
|---|---|
| Hook | First sentence, what is on screen at frame 0, seconds until the claim lands |
| Beats | Each beat with start time and one line on what it does (claim, proof, step, turn, close) |
| Pacing | Length, words per second, how often the picture changes |
| Visuals | What is on screen for each spoken line, and which are real footage vs made |
| Close | The ask, and what the viewer gets for it |
| Why it spread | The share driver (social currency, utility, relatability, perspective flip, arousal) and who would send it |
| Portable | The topic, angle or structure you can reuse |

## Rules
- Report only what you saw and heard. Mark every guess as a guess.
- Retention and audience data are not public. Never claim why people stayed or left.
- If the reel is an outlier for its account (see outlier-finder), say by how much. If you do not know, say so.
- End with one line: "Steal the shape: ...". It must not contain a quoted line from the reel.
